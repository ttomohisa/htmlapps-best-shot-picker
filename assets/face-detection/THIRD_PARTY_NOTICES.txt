Optional local face-position detection

1. @tensorflow-models/face-detection 1.0.3 (Apache-2.0)
   Copyright 2024 Google LLC. All Rights Reserved.
   https://github.com/tensorflow/tfjs-models/tree/master/face-detection
   https://www.npmjs.com/package/@tensorflow-models/face-detection/v/1.0.3
   The original UMD bundle and Google Apache header are preserved byte-for-byte.
   Exact npm archive and bundle SHA-256 pins are recorded in manifest.json.
   The MediaPipe JavaScript/WASM runtime is not included. Only runtime:'tfjs'
   with the CPU backend is used.

2. MediaPipe face-detection TFJS short, version 1 (Apache-2.0)
   https://www.kaggle.com/models/mediapipe/face-detection/tfJs/short/1
   manifest.json records the official archive, model and weight digests plus
   the reviewed Kaggle model-instance/version/license metadata. The full-range
   model is not included. No photographs or personal files are distributed.

3. TensorFlow.js 4.22.0 (Apache-2.0)
   https://github.com/tensorflow/tfjs/tree/tfjs-v4.22.0
   The face feature reuses the single tfjs/runtime asset already embedded by
   the app. The complete license and bundled third-party notices are retained
   under assets/embedding and embedded with that dependency. The complete
   Apache-2.0 text also accompanies these face assets.
